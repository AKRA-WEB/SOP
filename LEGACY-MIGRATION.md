- 27 root-level JPG screenshots

## Migration rule

Legacy screenshots remain untouched during the pilot. A legacy page or image
is retired only after its replacement task SOP has passed factual review,
employee comprehension review, and asset/privacy checks. The final migration
map will record each legacy section as replaced, retained, or explicitly
excluded.
